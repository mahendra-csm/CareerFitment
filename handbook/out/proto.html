Dimension 01 of 08

Personality

“How do I naturally work and decide?”

In simple words Your personality is your natural style, like being left- or right-handed. You can use both hands, but one feels easier. This dimension finds the style that feels easiest for you, so you can choose work where you don't have to fight yourself every day.

What it measures

Where you get your energy, how you take in information, how you make decisions and how you like to plan. These patterns shape how you prefer to work with others, handle tasks and make choices.

The framework

Personality-type preferences in four pairs, widely known as MBTI-style typing (from Carl Jung's work, developed by Myers and Briggs). Some stages also look at the five everyday traits psychologists call the Big Five.

Why it matters

Different work settings suit different patterns. Knowing your personality helps you pick paths where you contribute in your natural way, enjoy the work and grow steadily.

ISFJ“The Defender” · quiet, practical, caring, organised

Each slider shows which side you lean to and how clearly. Your four letters together make your type, for example ISFJ. Illustrative.

Example

Before a school trip, Ravi makes a checklist a week early (Judging); Meera packs the night before and enjoys deciding on the spot (Perceiving). Neither is wrong. But Ravi may enjoy project management, and Meera may thrive in fast-changing roles like journalism or event work.

How to use your dashboard

Your dashboard is where the report turns into action. Use it in this order the first time, then come back every week.

01 Sign in

Go to careerfitment.onegrasp.com and sign in with the email and password you used for the assessment.

02 Open your report

On the home screen tap Open my full report. Use Download to save the PDF.

03 Explore your results

In the left menu, open Your results: profile, 8 dimensions, best-fit fields, how you think, my plan.

04 Pick a tool

Choose a tool from Explore in the left menu, starting with your top career field in the Career Library.

05 Do the missions

Each tool chapter in this book ends with a mission card. Tick tasks off as you finish them on the dashboard.

06 Track your progress

Open My Progress to see lessons done, time spent and your streak; collect certificates as you finish courses.
