What your report shows for each one
PePersonality. Your four-letter type, a score for each preference, strengths and next steps.
InCareer Interests. Your three-letter interest code (Holland's RIASEC) and how strongly you lean to each type.
MiMultiple Intelligences. All eight intelligences ranked (Gardner), with your top two named.
EqEmotional Intelligence. Your EQ level, a score for each of four areas and what to practise first.
LsLearning Style. Your preferred style, with revision tips that suit it.
MoMotivators. Your core driver and how your other drivers rank.
StStrengths. Your two signature strengths and the fields that reward them.
ApAptitude. Your overall aptitude, your sharpest area and practice tips for exams.
Built onPersonality-type preferences (Jung; Myers and Briggs) · Holland's RIASEC interests · Gardner's multiple intelligences · Emotional intelligence (Salovey and Mayer; Goleman) · Learning preferences similar to VARK · Work-values research · Strengths-based psychology · Standard reasoning tests
